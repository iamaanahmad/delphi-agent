One auditable loop

Evidence first. Execution last.

Most agents begin with an opinion. Settlement Edge begins with a declared source and exact settlement rule, then makes execution earn its way through the rest of the pipeline.

01

Verify

Reject malformed, stale, future-dated, or off-rule evidence.

02

Estimate

Shrink confidence when sources disagree or the signal weakens.

03

Quote

Price several tiny sizes on the competition LMSR curve.

04

Gate

Cap spend, bankroll share, price impact, and slippage.

05

Record

Hash-link the fact, price, policy, and final decision.

Fail-closed by design

A weak edge becomes no trade.

Dry-run is the default. Live execution needs two explicit switches, a registered and funded competition wallet, passing evidence, and an executable quote inside every cap.
