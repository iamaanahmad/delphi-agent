# Competition cutoffs

Checked against the official competition materials on August 20, 2026. All times below are UTC.

| Competition step | Confirmed cutoff or status | Operator action |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet registration | August 23, 2026 at 23:59 UTC | Register the exact trading wallet through DoraHacks before the deadline. The event card calls this a submission deadline, but the competition instructions require only the wallet registration. No GitHub repository, code review, video, or BUIDL submission is required. |
| Competition trading | August 23, 2026 at 23:59 UTC | Treat this as the hard stop for new competition orders. DoraHacks describes the window as August 10 to August 24, while its exact event timestamp ends at 23:59 UTC on August 23. |
| Final judging | No exact UTC cutoff is published | Wait for all competition markets to settle. Gensyn then reviews final P&L and eligibility before confirming the leaderboard and prizes. Results can be published after the trading close. |

The official [DoraHacks competition page](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/delphi-agent-competition/detail) supplies the exact event timestamp, says that nothing beyond the registered wallet must be submitted, and places final review after settlement. The official [Gensyn competition reference](https://github.com/gensyn-ai/gensyn-delphi-skills/blob/main/reference/competition.md) confirms that registration happens through DoraHacks, the registered wallet is the leaderboard identity, and ranking uses competition P&L. The Gensyn reference does not publish a separate calendar cutoff, so it does not override the DoraHacks timestamp.

Numeric minimum activity requirements and tie-breaking rules are mentioned but not published in either checked source. They remain unknown and must not be inferred.
